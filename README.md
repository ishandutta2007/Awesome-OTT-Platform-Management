## Top OTT Platform Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Over-The-Top Video Streaming, Content Management, Monetization, Live & VOD Delivery*  

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **OTT Platform Management**. These tools enable content owners, broadcasters, and creators to launch branded streaming services, manage video libraries, handle live and on-demand delivery, implement monetization (SVOD/AVOD/TVOD), build multi-device apps, and operate end-to-end OTT experiences.



**Examples** include Brightcove, Kaltura, Dacast, Muvi, VPlayed, Uscreen, Vimeo OTT, GudSho, JW Player OTT, and Setplex (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted video platforms, live streaming servers, federated video hosting, media CMS, and open streaming engines — ideal for creators, media companies, educators, and developers seeking full control over their video infrastructure and data.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Media servers**: nginx-rtmp, MistServer, and other RTMP/HLS engines for custom ingest and delivery.

- **Player & frontend libraries**: Video.js, hls.js, and open players that integrate with self-hosted backends.

- **Federated & P2P**: PeerTube ecosystem tools and ActivityPub video integrations.

- **Transcoding & processing**: FFmpeg-based pipelines and open media processing workflows.

- Community **OTT CMS**, **live event platforms**, and **white-label streaming** prototypes on GitHub.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine a **media server** (SRS, Ant Media, or OvenMediaEngine) for ingest and delivery, **MediaCMS or PeerTube** for content management and discovery, **Owncast** for live + chat, open players, and optional monetization layers to create a complete self-hosted OTT platform.
